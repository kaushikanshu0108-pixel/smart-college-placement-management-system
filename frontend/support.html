💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why am I marked "Ineligible" for a drive? The automated eligibility matrix checks CGPA thresholds, maximum allowed active backlogs, application deadlines, and mandatory skill prerequisites in real time. Click "Details" on any drive card to view your live verification checklist.
Q: How do I update my CGPA or clear backlogs? Academic metrics are synchronized with the university examination records. Submit a support ticket under "Student Profile / CGPA Correction" with your grade sheet attached.
Q: How will I be notified about interview rounds? When coordinators schedule an interview round, details appear immediately on your Student Portal Overview tab and an in-app alert is generated with meeting link or venue details.
